About Bonak

An Aussie fox with variety energy.

BonakVT streams on both Twitch and YouTube, bringing a mix of League of Legends, Overwatch, variety gaming, single-player adventures, reactions, and chill chat time.

The whole vibe is meant to feel welcoming: stylish, bright, slightly fox-like, and fun without tipping into childish. That means clean shapes, bold contrast, warm gold accents, icy blue touches, and lots of personality.
