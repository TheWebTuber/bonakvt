About Me

G'day! It is I, Bonak! I'm an Aussie fox VTuber who loves playing League of Legends, jumping into variety games, reacting to random stuff, and chatting with you all. You'll also find games like Overwatch and single-player adventures when I feel like mixing things up.

I stream on both Twitch and YouTube, and turn some of my favourite stream moments into videos, Shorts, and clips. Expect gaming, reactions, a little chaos, and plenty of chances to hang out. If that sounds like your kind of thing, come say hello!

When Do I Stream?

I usually aim for two streams a day from Monday to Friday: around 2 PM–4 PM AEST and 7 PM–9 PM AEST. These are my usual times and can change, so check Twitch or YouTube for the latest live streams and updates.

What Can You Expect?

League of Legends, variety and single-player gaming, fun reactions, and a good chat. Whether you're watching live or catching up later, you can find stream VODs, videos, and Shorts on my YouTube channel, plus clips on Instagram.

Ultimate Goal!

My goal is to become monetized on Twitch and YouTube, and hopefully turn content creation into something I can do full-time one day!

I can't wait to see where this adventure takes us. "Let's make unforgettable memories together!"
